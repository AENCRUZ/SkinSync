About SKINSYNC

SKINSYNC is an AI-assisted platform for organizing skin concerns and preparing for professional dermatology care.

What SKINSYNC Does

Understand

Organize what you're noticing about a skin concern into clear, useful information.

Prepare

Build a consultation summary so your dermatologist visit starts with the full picture.

Connect

Find nearby dermatologists and clinics, and book an appointment.

Manage

Track prescribed skin medications, schedules, and doses over time.

Safety First

SKINSYNC does not provide definitive diagnoses, does not prescribe medication, and does not replace a dermatologist. It is designed to support — not substitute — professional dermatology care, by helping you organize information and prepare for your visit.
