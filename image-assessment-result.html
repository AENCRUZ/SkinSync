Image Observations

Only visible, observable characteristics are noted:

  • Slightly raised, reddened patch of skin
  • Irregular but well-defined border
  • No visible open wound or bleeding

General Information

Different skin concerns can share similar visible characteristics, such as redness or raised texture. Visual observation alone cannot determine an underlying cause.

Things to Discuss With Your Dermatologist

  • When the patch first appeared and how it has changed
  • The new laundry detergent used recently
  • Any associated itching, pain, or spreading
This is general information, not a diagnosis or prescription. A dermatologist can properly evaluate this concern.
