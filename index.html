Our Approach

Designed to Support, Not Replace, Dermatologists

SKINSYNC provides general information, consultation preparation, and organizational support. It does not provide a definitive diagnosis or prescribe medication.

  • General Skin Information

    Plain-language context about visible skin characteristics.

  • Consultation Preparation

    A clear summary to bring to your dermatologist.

  • Dermatologist Recommendations

    Help finding qualified professionals near you.
